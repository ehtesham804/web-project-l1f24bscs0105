Workout Protocols & Rules

Athlete Warmup Sequence

  1. 5 minutes dynamic myofascial tissue rolling
  2. Controlled thoracic and hip mobility rotations
  3. Activation drills: Glute bridges and band pull-aparts
  4. Progressive warm-up sets with barbell
  5. Working compound sets execution

Facility Etiquette

  • Sanitize and wipe down machines after every set
  • Barbell collars are mandatory across all stations
  • Rerack dumbbells in ascending weight sequence
  • Appropriate rubber-soled athletic footwear required
